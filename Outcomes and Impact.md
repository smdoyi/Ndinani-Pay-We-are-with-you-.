Outcomes & Impact

Usability Testing

We conducted usability testing with 10 elderly and visually impaired participants at the local community hall. Key observations:

. Users were able to complete a full transaction using only voice prompts in an average of 3 minutes
.80% of participants said the language options made them feel more confident using the app independently
.Visually impaired testers reported the voice confirmation step was the most valuable feature, as it reduced fear of making mistakes

Pilot testing is ongoing; adoption metrics will be tracked in the next phase.

Qualitative Feedback

> "For the first time, I didn't need my grandson to check my balance." ,  Pilot user, Eastern Cape branch

> "The voice confirmation gave me confidence I wasn't sending money to the wrong person." , Visually impaired pilot user

 Challenges Encountered

. Some elderly users needed multiple in-branch sessions before feeling confident using the app alone
.Voice recognition accuracy dropped in noisy environments, requiring further tuning
. Balancing simple language with necessary banking terminology required several rounds of testing

 Next Steps

. Expand language support beyond isiXhosa, English, and Afrikaans as the app scales to new regions
.Improve offline/low-bandwidth functionality for rural users
. Partner with accessibility advocacy groups for formal WCAG compliance testing
