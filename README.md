 Ndinani Pay — Business Analyst Case Study

Ndinani Pay is a voice-guided fintech platform designed for elderly and visually impaired users who are excluded from mainstream banking apps due to language barriers and inaccessible interfaces. This repository documents the business analysis work behind the project — from problem definition through to requirements tracking and impact evaluation.

## My Role

I led the business analysis for this project, including stakeholder analysis, user research, requirements gathering, and process mapping to ensure the app was built around the real needs of elderly and visually impaired users.

## Why This Project Matters

Millions of elderly and visually impaired people are excluded from digital financial independence , not because the technology doesn't exist, but because it isn't designed with them in mind. This project shows how business analysis through user research, requirements definition, and stakeholder alignment can turn an overlooked problem into an inclusive, working solution.
